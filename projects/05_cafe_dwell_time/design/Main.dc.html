Observed and interpolated are separate columns. frames_detected and frames_held are stored apart, so a service figure that is partly held through occlusion can never read as one that was fully observed — Room 5 is 88 detected / 6 held; Room 1 is 38 / 33.
Read the limits: occupancy is a detection result and dwell is a tracking result — a broken identity becomes two visitors who each stayed half as long, so every visit carries a continuity score. No faces, no re-identification across rooms or sessions.
